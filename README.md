## Alchemy of Souls

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Alchemy of Souls is a K-drama set in the fictional country of Daeho, about an elite assassin named Nak-su whose soul is trapped in the body of a blind woman, Mu-deok, after using a forbidden soul-shifting spell. She becomes the servant and secret magic master to Jang Uk, a young nobleman with a troubled past. The synopsis follows their relationship, which evolves from master-servant and teacher-student to romance, as they navigate the dangerous politics of Daeho and work to restore Nak-su's powers and unlock Jang Uk's full potentials.
